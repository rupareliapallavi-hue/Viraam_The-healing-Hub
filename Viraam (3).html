About Viraam

Viraam is a pause in a loud life.

We began with a simple belief: most of us are not short of effort, we are short of rest. Our sessions combine Reiki, chakra work, sound and breath so your body and mind can settle.

Each session is unhurried, private and shaped around you. We do not diagnose or replace medical care. We support it with calm, attention and practice you can take home.
